01. About Me

Building dependable software and AI you can actually understand.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Asian University for Women (expected 2027), originally from Cambodia. I started with C, Python and full-stack web development at E2STEM, and went on to build a PHP and MySQL room booking system during my internship at ISI GROUP.

Today my work sits between software engineering and machine learning research. I fine-tune transformer models such as BERT and RoBERTa for phishing and hate speech detection, and use SHAP and LIME so their decisions can be explained, not just trusted.

Outside of code, I teach programming as a university Teaching Assistant, map migration stories in a Digital Humanities lab, and advocate for women in technology as a STEM Sister ambassador and former Cambodia Country Representative.
